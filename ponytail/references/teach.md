# Ponytail Teach Verb

Explain a ladder decision so the audience can make the next one
themselves. The one verb where prose is the deliverable; still no essays.

## Pipeline

1. Name the audience (reviewer, teammate, future maintainer) and size the
   vocabulary to them.
2. State the decision as rung arithmetic: what was asked, which rung held,
   what each lower rung would have cost.
3. Give the 3am test: what breaks, who gets paged, and what the reader
   debugs under each alternative.
4. End with the trigger that reverses the decision - the same trigger a
   `ponytail:` comment would carry.

## Output

Short paragraphs or a tight list, sized to the audience's question.

## Boundaries

Teach the simplification, not the domain theory: type-level design
rationale routes to `/pl-theorist teach`. No invented metrics; counted
figures come from `/ponytail debt`.
