# Draft Paper Help Verb

Display this reference card when invoked. One-shot: print the card and stop;
the pipeline, files, and persisted state stay untouched.

## Verbs

| Verb | Trigger | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **design** | `/draft-paper design` | Position a spark or shaped idea: contradiction framing, experiment plan, award assessment. Stages 1-2. |
| **build** | `/draft-paper build` | Build the complete draft from shaped, partial, or full results. Stages 2-8. Default. |
| **refactor** | `/draft-paper refactor` | Retarget an existing draft to a new format or venue, claims preserved. Stages 3-8, abbreviated. |
| **rebut** | `/draft-paper rebut` | Answer reviews: rebuttal draft or revision letter, AC-facing summary. Stage 9. |

No explicit verb: a rough idea routes to `design`, results to `build`,
reviews to `rebut`.

## Formats

`short` (6-page idea paper), `full` (conference paper), `workshop`,
`journal`, `survey`, `demo`. Section structure per `section-guide`; venue
rules per `venue-standards`.

## Gates

Human approval is required three times: the research plan (stage 1), the
evidence ledger (stage 2), and the final draft (stage 8).
